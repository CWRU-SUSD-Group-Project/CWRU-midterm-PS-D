## Operating terms

- Staff agency rate is $16 per staffed hour, including employer costs, insurance, paid breaks, and agency relief. It supplies shifts of up to eight hours per person and relief cover. Family members are not available as unpaid staff.
- Card processing is 3% of sales. No per-transaction fee applies.
- Public liability insurance is $100 per month.
- Cleaning and consumable supplies are $80 per month.
- Water, electricity, and internet are included in rent. There are no other required monthly charges in the quote.
- The landlord requires a $2,500 refundable security deposit before handing over the keys. It is separate from rent. It is returned September 7 if there is no damage or unpaid rent.
- The lease does not require all three rent payments upfront. Pay first-month rent and the deposit before opening. Suppliers accept weekly orders without a minimum after the initial order.
- Customers pay when buying. No credit sales. Goods can be replenished weekly from receipts.
