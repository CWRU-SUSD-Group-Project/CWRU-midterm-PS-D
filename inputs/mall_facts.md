# Maple Mall leasing sheet

Maple Mall is an enclosed mall in a mid-sized college town. Of its 100 shop units, 70 are empty. The available pop-up is Unit 42, a 400-square-foot inline space near the central court. The lease runs June 1 through August 31, 2027. The business has $20,000 available and cannot borrow more for this project.

Rent is $2,500 per month, paid on the first of each month. The mall is open 10 AM to 9 PM every day. Traffic counts in foot_traffic.csv are a typical week counted at the central-court corridor beside Unit 42 during May 2027. Each row covers the hour starting at the listed hour. Counts are passing visits, not unique people; do not add hourly samples more than once per week. June has 30 days, July 31, and August 31.

Northfield College, with 4,800 enrolled students, is a ten-minute walk away. Summer classes run through August. A bus stop and 420 free parking spaces serve the mall.

## Tenant rules

- All pop-ups must be staffed and trading during every mall opening hour, seven days a week.
- One person must be present at a time. A second person is permitted. Rosters must allow legal meal/rest coverage; the staffing supplier provides relief within its hourly quote.
- Food sales need a food permit; non-food retail needs a general vendor permit.
- No cooking with open flames. Existing water and standard electrical outlets are included.
- Signs stay inside the leased footprint. Mall approval for signs takes two business days.
- Tenants remove their stock and displays by August 31. No additional exit charge applies if the space is left clean.

## Mall directory notes

Anchor tenants are Harlow's department store in the east wing and ValueHome in the west wing. Harlow's final trading day is July 31, 2027. The leasing office expects corridor visits in August to be about 30% below the May weekly pattern. ValueHome remains open throughout the lease.

The library pickup locker and college bus shelter operate all summer. Mall email listings and the central noticeboard are available to tenants without charge.
