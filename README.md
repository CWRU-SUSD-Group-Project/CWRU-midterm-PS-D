# Pop-up in a Dead Mall

Maple Mall is 70% empty. A pop-up space costs $2,500 a month for three months. Your team has $20,000 total.

Pick one pop-up idea and prove it breaks even by month 3.

## Inputs

- inputs/mall_facts.md: the mall and lease information.
- inputs/foot_traffic.csv: visitors by day and hour.
- inputs/competitors.csv: businesses in the mall.
- inputs/shopper_survey.csv: shopper responses.
- inputs/cost_list.md: vendor and operating costs.

## Roles

| Role | Your file | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer Analyst | roles/01_customer_analyst/customers.md | Who walks by and when, what they would buy, and who the competitors are. |
| Finance Analyst | roles/02_finance_analyst/finance.md | Startup cost, monthly cost, sales needed, and break-even date. |
| Risk Manager | roles/03_risk_manager/risks.md | Staffing, supplies, mall rules, and the top three things that could go wrong. |
| Marketing Manager (leader) | roles/04_marketing_manager/marketing.md | How to get people to show up in month 1. |

## Rules

Hidden problems are in the inputs. Finding them is part of your score.
Use only files in inputs/. Prove your answer is right.

Work on your own role branch and edit only your role file. Leave inputs/, shared/, AGENTS.md, and other role folders alone. Read AGENTS.md for joining and finishing instructions. The leader creates final/ after all four roles are done. Team members review the final work before it is merged.

## Final work

The leader creates final/plan.md: one page with the idea, the break-even date with math, the top three risks, and launch marketing.
$20,000 is all the cash you have. Everything you pay before you break even must fit inside it. Show a month-by-month cash table.
The leader also creates final/presentation_outline.md for a five-minute presentation.
