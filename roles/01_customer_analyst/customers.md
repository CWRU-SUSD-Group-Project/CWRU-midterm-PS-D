# Customer Analyst

## Findings

**Customers and demand:** In `inputs/shopper_survey.csv`, 60 of 100 respondents selected cheap snacks, compared with 25 for phone accessories, 10 for art and craft bundles, and 5 for used books. Snack interest appears across all five age groups, with 12 snack-interested respondents in each. Their stated maximum prices range from $3 to $6.

All survey responses are from Saturday. These results show stated interest among surveyed Saturday shoppers; they do not establish weekday demand or actual purchase rates.

**Location and timing:** `inputs/mall_facts.md` places Unit 42 beside the central court, near a college with summer classes, a bus stop, and a library pickup locker. College students are a potential audience, but the survey supports considering multiple age groups.

Summing each hourly observation once in `inputs/foot_traffic.csv` gives 5,531 passing visits per typical week. Saturday has 1,529 and Sunday has 1,463: together, 2,992 ÷ 5,531 = approximately 54% of weekly visits. Both weekend days peak from 2–3 PM. Each weekday peaks from 6–7 PM. These counts are passing visits, not unique people or guaranteed customers.

**Competition:** `inputs/competitors.csv` lists three snack competitors: Pretzel Porch, with a $5.50 average purchase price; Sugar Corner, $4; and Pop & Go, $3.50. These prices overlap the survey’s snack price limits, so snack demand alone does not prove an unmet need. Average purchase prices may also represent different products or quantities.

**Demand risk:** According to `inputs/mall_facts.md`, Harlow’s closes July 31 and August corridor visits are expected to be approximately 30% below the May pattern. The team should reduce August traffic assumptions accordingly.

## Recommendation

Evaluate a packaged-snack pop-up selling at $3 per serving. This price is within every snack-interested respondent’s stated maximum and below the three snack competitors’ listed average purchase prices. Affordability is a proposed selling point that still needs testing.

Prioritize weekend afternoon promotion and stock availability, with weekday evening promotion as a secondary opportunity. The shop must still trade from 10 AM to 9 PM every day under the mall rules.

At the quoted $1.25 cost per serving and assuming a 3% card fee, each $3 sale leaves $3 − $1.25 − $0.09 = $1.66 toward rent, staffing, and other costs (`inputs/cost_list.md`). This is not net profit. Recommend proceeding only if Finance can demonstrate realistic sales volumes, break-even by month three, and cash requirements within $20,000.

## Handoff

- **Finance Team :** Test the $3 price and $1.66 contribution per serving. Calculate required sales and the implied share of passing visits that must become purchases. Do not treat 60% survey interest as a purchase conversion rate. Include the August traffic decline.
- **Marketing Team :** Emphasize affordability to a broad audience, including nearby students. Prioritize weekend afternoons and weekday evenings. Consider the free mall email listings and noticeboard described in `inputs/mall_facts.md`.
- **Risk Managers:** Address Saturday-only survey coverage, existing snack competition, uncertain purchase conversion, and lower August traffic. Plan staffing for all
